veriT proofs Carcara rejected over int/real subtyping
======================================================

653 of the 23,328 veriT benchmarks in the round-four SMT-LIB run failed with

    sort error: expected 'Real', got 'Int'

from Carcara's parser, before any checking began. 650 of them check *valid*
after the fix (round six); the remaining 3 are listed separately at the end and
fail for unrelated reasons.

The defect was Carcara's, not veriT's. `--allow-int-real-subtyping` was consulted
for the operators whose argument sorts are fixed and numeric (+, *, /, the order
comparisons) but not for the positions where the sorts merely have to agree with
each other: the arguments of = and distinct, the branches of an ite, and the
arguments of an uninterpreted function. veriT writes an integer literal on one
side of an equality whose other side is real-sorted, which is legal under the
flag. The fix is confined to those three polymorphic positions, so (div 1.0 2.0)
is still rejected.

The same defect had appeared in an earlier three-logic round on 194 QF_LRA
proofs, where it was misattributed to lia_generic residue.
